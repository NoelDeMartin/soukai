# Conventions

`soukai-bis` is the unified rewrite of `soukai` and `soukai-solid`. These 2 are considered "legacy" packages at this point, and that's why they may not follow my [project conventions](https://github.com/NoelDeMartin/scripts/blob/main/CONVENTIONS.md) completely. The docs project (and the `deploy.yml` workflow) also needs to be updated once the rewrite is completed.
